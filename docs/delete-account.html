Delete your Bolo English account

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open Bolo English and sign in.
  2. Open the Me tab.
  3. In the account card at the top, tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with Yes, delete my account. Email accounts are asked for the password first.
Your account and everything saved online are deleted straight away.

Option 2: by email, without the app

Send an email from the address you use for Bolo English, with the subject Delete my Bolo English account:

Email boloenglish.app@gmail.com

We delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and reply when it is done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address, name and user ID.
  • Every profile in the account and all its progress saved online (lessons, stars, XP, streaks, marks, word lists).

We keep nothing after deletion. Data saved only on a phone (for example when the app is used without an account) is removed by deleting the profile in the app or uninstalling the app.
