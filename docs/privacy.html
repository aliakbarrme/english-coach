Who we are

Bolo English is a family app for learning and speaking English, made by Ali Akbar (BD Webhut), Bangladesh. Questions about privacy: boloenglish.app@gmail.com.

Who the app is for

Bolo English is made for the whole family: young children, teens and adults. Children learn in profiles that a parent or guardian sets up. Accounts are meant to be created by a parent or guardian, not by a child. If you think a child created an account without a parent, write to us and we will delete it.

Microphone and speaking practice

The microphone is used only when you press a speak or record button. For "speaking check", the app uses the speech recognition built into your browser or phone (on Android this is Google's speech service) to turn what you say into text. We never receive or store that audio. Recordings made with the "Record" button stay on your phone.

What we do not collect

We do not collect location, contacts, phone numbers, advertising IDs, device IDs or browsing history. We do not use analytics, crash tracking or advertising tools. There are no ads.

Services that help run the app

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore, data stored in Mumbai, India) keeps accounts and backed-up progress, only if you sign in. Firebase privacy.
  • GitHub Pages serves the app's files. Like any web host, it may keep short technical logs such as IP addresses for security. GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts provides the app's fonts.

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with anyone else, except if the law requires it.

Children's privacy

We follow children's privacy laws such as COPPA (USA) and GDPR (EU). For children the app has no ads, no purchases, no chat or social features and no location. A child's profile holds only a nickname, an emoji, the age group and learning progress. Parents can delete a profile or the whole account at any time.

How long we keep data, and deleting it

Data on your phone stays until you delete the profile or uninstall the app. Data in your account stays until you delete the account.

  • In the app: open Me, go to the account card and tap Delete account. Your account and all progress saved online are deleted straight away.
  • Without the app: see how to delete your account, or email boloenglish.app@gmail.com from your account's email. We delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Online, each account can read and change only its own data.

Your choices and rights

You can use the app without an account, download a backup of a profile from Me → Backup, change or delete profiles, and delete your account. You can also email us to see, correct or delete your data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
